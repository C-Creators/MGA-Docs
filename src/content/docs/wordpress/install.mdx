## Quick install <Badge text="5 minutes" variant="success" size="small" />

<Steps>
1. **Download the zip** from your MyGPT Assistants dashboard → **Embed → WordPress** tab. The download link is signed and ties to your account.

2. **Upload to WordPress.** In your WP admin go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**, choose the `mga-wp.zip` you just downloaded, click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.

3. **Open the plugin.** A new **MGA WP** entry appears in the WordPress sidebar. Open it — you'll land on the Dashboard.

4. **Paste your API key.** Open **Settings**, paste the `mga_…` API key from your MyGPT Assistants account, and click **Connect**. The plugin validates the key against `/api/v1/user/me` and shows your account name on success.

5. **Sync your bots.** Open **Bots** and press **Sync bots**. Every bot from your MyGPT Assistants account is imported into the local table (`mga_wp_bots`). After this, the **Bot Workspace** dropdown in the sidebar is populated with all your bots.
</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Bot Workspace dropdown is everything">
  Every settings page in the plugin operates on whichever bot is selected
  in the **Select bot** dropdown. The dropdown drives the URL
  (`/bots/<id>/…`); the URL drives the form load; the form save targets
  that bot's row only.
</Aside>

— customizer fields, preview, and the embed shortcode all change with it.

## Auto-updates

Once installed, the plugin polls your MyGPT Assistants account for new
releases every 12 hours (via the `plugin-update-checker` library). When a
newer version is available you'll see an **Update available** notice on
WP's **Plugins** page exactly like any other plugin.

<Aside type="tip" title="Force an update check">
  Don't want to wait? On **Plugins**, click the **"Check for updates"**
  link next to MGA WP and WordPress will refresh immediately.
</Aside>
