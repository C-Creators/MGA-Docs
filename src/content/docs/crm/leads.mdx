## Turn Conversations into Customers <Badge text="CRM Feature" variant="note" size="small" />

Leads are prospects captured by your AI assistant before they become full contacts. Your assistant qualifies them during conversation — gathering name, email, interest level, and budget — and creates a structured lead record in your CRM automatically.

![Leads management](../../../assets/screenshots/crm-leads.png)

## Leads vs. Contacts

| | Leads | Contacts |
|-|-------|---------|
| **Who** | Prospects being qualified | Known customers/clients |
| **Created by** | AI assistant during conversation | Manual import, form, or conversion from lead |
| **Pipeline** | Leads pipeline | Deals pipeline |
| **Goal** | Qualify and convert | Manage ongoing relationship |

## How Leads Are Created

Your AI assistant creates lead records automatically when it detects qualifying intent in a conversation:

1. **Visitor initiates chat** on your website or channel
2. **AI detects buying/inquiry intent** ("I'm looking for pricing", "Can you help me with X")
3. **AI qualifies** — asks for name, email, company, and key interest details
4. **Lead created in CRM** with all collected data and the full conversation transcript

<Aside type="tip" title="AI Lead Qualification">
The assistant uses AI intent classification to detect when a conversation has lead potential. It doesn't rely on keywords — it understands context.
</Aside>

## Lead Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Name** | Prospect's full name |
| **Email** | Contact email address |
| **Company** | Organization name |
| **Source** | Channel (website, WhatsApp, Instagram, etc.) |
| **Status** | New / Contacted / Qualified / Converted / Lost |
| **Score** | AI-assigned qualification score |
| **Notes** | AI summary of conversation |
| **Assigned To** | Team member responsible |

## Lead Pipeline

Leads progress through stages:

```
New  →  Contacted  →  Qualified  →  Converted  →  [Contact created]
  ↓
Lost (not interested or unresponsive)
```

When a lead is **Converted**, a Contact is automatically created with all the lead's information. The lead record is archived.

## Managing Leads

<Steps>
1. **Navigate to CRM → Leads**

2. **Review new leads** — sorted by creation date with AI score

3. **Open a lead** to see the conversation transcript and collected details

4. **Assign to a team member** for follow-up

5. **Update the stage** as you progress (Contacted → Qualified)

6. **Convert to Contact** when ready to start a formal relationship
</Steps>

## Lead Scoring

The AI assigns each lead a score based on:
- Completeness of information (email, company, etc.)
- Expressed interest level
- Budget or timeline signals
- Channel quality (website vs. cold social)

Higher scores = higher priority leads to follow up with first.
