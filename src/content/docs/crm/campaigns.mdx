## Campaigns vs. Sequences

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Campaigns">
    **One-time sends** to a segment of contacts.
    
    - Send a single email or message to a group
    - Target by segment (e.g., "All leads from last month")
    - Track opens, clicks, and replies
    - Best for announcements, promotions, newsletters
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Sequences">
    **Multi-step automated drips** triggered by enrollment.
    
    - Define a series of messages with delays between them
    - Contacts move through automatically
    - Stop when they reply or convert
    - Best for lead nurturing, onboarding, follow-up series
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Creating a Campaign

<Steps>
1. **Navigate to CRM → Campaigns**

2. **Click "New Campaign"**

3. **Choose your audience:**
   - Select a Segment (e.g., "Hot Leads", "New Contacts")
   - Or choose individual contacts

4. **Write your message:**
   - Email subject and body
   - Or a message template for WhatsApp/Telegram

5. **Schedule or send immediately**

6. **Review the preview** and confirm
</Steps>

## Creating a Sequence

<Steps>
1. **Navigate to CRM → Sequences**

2. **Click "New Sequence"**

3. **Add steps:**
   - Step 1: Day 0 — Welcome message
   - Step 2: Day 3 — Follow-up with value
   - Step 3: Day 7 — Offer or call to action

4. **Set enrollment criteria:**
   - Manual enrollment
   - Auto-enroll when contact matches a segment
   - Auto-enroll when a trigger fires (e.g., form submitted)

5. **Activate the sequence**
</Steps>

## Message Types

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Email" icon="email">
    Full HTML email with your brand template. Supports personalization tokens (name, company, etc.).
  </Card>
  <Card title="WhatsApp" icon="phone">
    Text message sent to contacts' WhatsApp numbers. Requires WhatsApp integration active.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Telegram" icon="comment">
    Message via your Telegram bot integration.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Personalization

Use tokens in your message content to personalize at scale:

| Token | Replaced With |
|-------|--------------|
| `{{name}}` | Contact's first name |
| `{{email}}` | Contact's email address |
| `{{company}}` | Contact's company |
| `{{assistant_name}}` | Your AI assistant's name |

## Campaign Analytics

After sending, the campaign detail page shows:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Sent** | Total messages delivered |
| **Opened** | Email open rate |
| **Clicked** | Link click rate |
| **Replied** | Contacts who responded |
| **Unsubscribed** | Contacts who opted out |

## Sequence Performance

For sequences, track each step:
- How many contacts are on each step
- Drop-off rate between steps
- Conversion events (reply, appointment booked, etc.)

<Aside type="tip" title="Effective Sequences">
The best sequences are short (3–5 steps), personal, and provide genuine value at each step. Avoid purely promotional messages — mix in helpful content.
</Aside>

## Audience Targeting

Campaigns integrate with Segments so you can target precisely:

- "All contacts from Instagram with no appointment"
- "Leads created in the last 30 days"
- "Contacts tagged 'VIP'"

See [Segments →](/crm/segments/) to build your audience.

## Compliance

<Aside type="caution" title="Email & Messaging Compliance">
- Always include an unsubscribe option in emails
- Only message contacts who have opted in to receive communications
- Respect WhatsApp's messaging policy for business accounts
- Contacts who unsubscribe are automatically excluded from future campaigns
</Aside>
