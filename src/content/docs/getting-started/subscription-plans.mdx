## Plans That Grow With You <Badge text="Billing" variant="note" size="small" />

MyGPTAssistants offers tiered subscription plans so you can start small and scale as your business grows. All plans include the core AI assistant features — plans differ by usage limits, number of assistants, and access to advanced features.

## Managing Your Subscription

<Steps>
1. **Navigate to Settings** (top-right menu → Settings)

2. **Click "Subscription"** in the left sidebar

3. **View your current plan** and usage

4. **Upgrade or change plan** using the plan cards

5. **Billing is managed via Stripe** — you'll be redirected to the billing portal for payment method changes
</Steps>

## Usage & Limits

Your plan includes a monthly message allowance. You can monitor your usage in:
- **Settings → Subscription** — current period usage and limit
- **Analytics** — message volume charts

<Aside type="caution" title="Approaching Your Limit">
If you approach your monthly message limit, you'll receive an email notification. Consider upgrading before you hit the limit to avoid service interruptions.
</Aside>

## Team Members

Growth and Business plans support multiple team members with role-based access:

| Role | Access Level |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full access to all settings, billing, and team management |
| **Admin** | Full access except billing |
| **Manager** | CRM access + can manage members' tasks |
| **Member** | CRM access per admin-set permissions |
| **Viewer** | Read-only CRM access |

## Annual Billing Discount

Switch to annual billing for a discount compared to monthly billing. Change billing cycle in **Settings → Subscription → Billing Portal**.

## Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings → Subscription**. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

<Aside type="note" title="Data on Cancellation">
Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation. You can export your contacts and conversations before the account is closed.
</Aside>
